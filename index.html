01 / About

The Band

Night Hog is Wade, Zach, and Jordan — three guys who probably should have grown out of this by now but show no signs of stopping.

Based out of Baton Rouge, they've been covering classic rock and getting rooms moving across Louisiana since before it was cool. Night Hog doesn't reinvent the wheel. They just make the wheel louder, sweatier, and more fun than you expected on a Tuesday.

From dive bars to backyard parties to venues that actually have stages, they bring the same energy every time: tight grooves, big riffs, and a genuine love for playing music for people who want to have a good time.

"Phenomenal." "Reeeeally good." "Surprisingly good looking."
— Real quotes. From real people. We're just as surprised as you are.
